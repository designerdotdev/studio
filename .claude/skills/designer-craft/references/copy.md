# Copy — the default values are the design

The yml defaults are what every visitor sees in the picker, on the canvas,
and on the installed site until the owner edits them. They are shipped
product, not placeholder. Write them the way a founder who has thought
hard about the product would.

## The voice

- **One imagined product, named, with a job.** Before writing a line,
  decide: what is it, who buys it, what does it replace, what is the one
  number it improves. Every default answers to that. (A block for the
  library still gets a product — invent one per block and keep it
  consistent across the block's fields.)
- **Specific beats confident.** "Invoices paid 11 days sooner" beats
  "Get paid faster". A feature says what changes for the user, with one
  concrete detail (a number, a named integration, a real edge case).
- **Short.** Headline ≤ 8 words; hero support copy ≤ 2 lines at 1440;
  feature description one sentence; card body one sentence + ≤ 2 figures;
  FAQ answer two sentences; testimonial ≤ 30 words with a full name, role,
  and company. If a block needs a paragraph to explain itself, the block
  is wrong.
- **Sentence case** for headings and buttons. No exclamation marks. No
  "!" in a CTA, ever.
- **Buttons name the outcome** — "Start free", "See pricing", "Book a
  demo", "Read the guide" — not "Primary button", "Learn more", "Submit".
- **Eyebrows are labels**, mono or small caps, two or three words: "Pricing",
  "How it works", "Customers".

## Banned phrases (lorem that passed spellcheck)

Rewrite on sight: *Welcome to our website · Build faster · Ship smarter ·
The all-in-one platform · Powerful features for modern teams · Take your X
to the next level · Seamless · Effortless · Unleash · Supercharge ·
Empower · Revolutionary · Cutting-edge · Best-in-class · World-class ·
Lorem ipsum · Coming soon · Primary button · Learn more · Get started
today! · Trusted by thousands · from 200+ reviews (unless the number is
part of a specific, named claim)*.

## Names, logos, people

- Company and product names are invented and *plausible*, one or two
  syllables, not puns: Ledger, Northwind, Halden, Marrow, Slate, Fjord.
- Logo strips use invented wordmarks set in the mono or display face
  (see imagery.md), never real brands and never the reference's.
- Testimonial authors get a full name, a real-sounding role, and a company
  in the invented world: "Priya Raman, Head of Finance, Northwind Studio".
- Numbers in stats are odd and specific (11 days, 4.9, 1,400, 99.98%),
  never round marketing numbers (10x, 1M+, 100%).

## Copy for a converted block (create-block)

The reference's copy is disposable. Keep its *shape* — how many words the
layout was designed for, whether the headline wraps to two lines, whether
the description is one sentence or two — and write new copy of the same
length for the invented product. Length parity is what keeps the layout
looking the way the reference did.
